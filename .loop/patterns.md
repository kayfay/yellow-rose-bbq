# Site Architecture & Design Invariants
1. Recipe Formulations are typically stored in Markdown or a data file.
2. We use standard ratios for Mexican Chorizo: Pork shoulder/butt with ~20-30% pork fatback.
3. Chile Paste Base: Rehydrated guajillo and ancho chiles blended with apple cider vinegar and fresh garlic.
4. Spice Blend: Mexican oregano, ground cumin, coriander, Mexican cinnamon, cloves, paprika, black pepper, kosher salt.
5. Verification Boundary: No task is complete without Maker-Checker execution output in the transcript.
6. Mathematical Conversions: Must be exact (e.g. lbs to oz/grams).
