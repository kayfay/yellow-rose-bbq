# Yellow Rose BBQ — Smokehouse Staff Roster & Shift Schedule

This document serves as the ground-truth operational staffing record for Yellow Rose BBQ. It feeds into the machine learning forecasting algorithms, shift transition models, and daily pitmaster briefings.

> [!NOTE]
> **Flexible Scheduling Policy**: Team members do not have fixed, rigid day-of-week calendar assignments (e.g. Freddy's, Elly's, or Nick's shifts vary week to week). Daily tips and briefings therefore provide role-specific guidance, station handoffs (e.g. Nick's 7:00 PM bus departure, Jake's 8:30 AM kitchen opening, and Trevor's 2:30 AM pit fire), and POS velocity metrics rather than hard-coded weekly calendar assumptions.

## 5. Critical Daily Operational Handoff Windows

1. **02:30 AM — Smoker Ignition**: Trevor arrives, fires the pit, trims briskets (<18% trim loss), rubs and seasons all meats.
2. **08:00 AM — Day Pit Handover**: Max arrives to relieve Trevor; verifies smoker internal temps, airflow dampers, and wrapping.
3. **08:30 AM — Kitchen Open**: Jake arrives at 8:30 AM sharp to fire burners and open the kitchen; Alyssa opens expo and preps fries and mac & cheese.
4. **02:00 PM — Afternoon Shift Influx**: Nick arrives for night prep; Allen takes mid-shift break.
5. **05:00–07:00 PM — Midday Exit & Pit Shutdown**: The Boss oversees pit coal shutdown (~6:00 PM); Jake departs (unless pulling a double); remaining meats rest at 140°F.
6. **07:00 PM — Transit Departure Bottleneck**: Nick departs for the bus. Freddy takes over primary meat slicing, grill, and line expo; Elly leads expo closing.
7. **Closing — Kitchen Lock-Down**: Freddy, Elly, and Allen complete station breakdown, fryer filtering, dish sanitation, and walk-in lockup.
