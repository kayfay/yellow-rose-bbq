## 1. Core Alignment Decisions (`/grill-me` Synthesis)

### A. Plain-English Operations + Financial KPIs (Zero-Jargon Rule)
All metrics, chart titles, tooltips, and daily briefs combine **Pit Operations (Yield & Prep)** and **Shop Financials (Sales & Meat Cost)** using everyday language:

| Banned Jargon Term | Plain-English Replacement Used in UI & Briefs |
| :--- | :--- |
| `DOW Base` / `Baseline` | **Typical [Day Name]** (e.g., *"Compared to a typical Friday"*) |
| `Z-Score` / `±2.5σ Anomaly` | **Unusual Rush** or **Slower Than Usual** |
| `COGS` / `Margin Bridge` | **Est. Meat Cost ($)** & **Sales Left After Meat Cost ($)** |
| `Depletion Velocity` | **Hourly Sales Pace (Orders / Lbs per Hour)** |
| `Weather Elasticity` / `Stadium Lift` | **Today's Weather Impact** & **Local Game Day Boost** |
| `Bullet Chart` / `Sparkline` | **Daily Target Progress Bar** & **Last 7 Days Mini-Trend** |

### B. Daily Shop Brief Delivery Format
1. **Top-of-Dashboard Callout Banner (`#daily-executive-brief-banner`):**
   - **What's Happening Today (The Insight):** 1 plain-English sentence explaining how sales, meat usage, or rush hours compare to a typical day and why (e.g., *"Friday sales are running +18% (+$640) ahead of a typical Friday because of a heavy 12:00 PM–2:00 PM Brisket and Spare Rib lunch rush."*).
   - **Recommended Next Step (The Action):** 1 concrete, practical action for the pit or counter (e.g., *"Pull 2 extra raw brisket packers (+28 lbs raw / ~11 lbs cooked) for tomorrow's smoke and keep slicing reserves ready before the 6:00 PM dinner rush."*).
2. **1-Click Printable 1-Page Daily Sheet (`#btn-print-daily-brief`):**
   - Formats the Daily Brief, Meat Prep Targets, and Sales vs. Meat Cost Summary into a clean, high-contrast 1-page printable sheet (`@media print`).
3. **Automated JSON Export (`clover_api/analytics/daily_executive_brief.json`):**
   - Generated by `scripts/daily_insight_engine.py` and bundled into `clover_api/analytics/payloads.js` (`window.PRELOADED_PAYLOADS.daily_executive_brief`).

### C. Visual Theme & Strict Viewport Behavior
- **Theme:** Clean Smokehouse Dark Theme by default + **Okabe-Ito Colorblind-Safe Palette** (`#E69F00` Amber, `#56B4E9` Sky Blue, `#009E73` Green, `#D55E00` Vermillion) + **1-Click Printable Daylight Mode** and Colorblind Simulation controls (`Standard`, `Deuteranopia`, `Protanopia`) at `375px`, `414px`, `768px`, and `1440px`.
- **Desktop (`>= 768px`):** High-clarity charts (no heavy gridlines or 3D clutter), inline target progress bars & 7-day mini-trends inside KPI cards, and plain-English hover tooltips.
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** **100% Static, Non-Interactive Chart Cards** (`staticPlot: true`, `pointer-events: none`, `touch-action: pan-y`) with **Pre-Baked High-Contrast Labels directly on the chart face** (`PEAK HOUR`, `QUIETEST HOUR`, `CURRENT PACE`) and `>= 16px` (`1rem`) text legibility.
