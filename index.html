Day 1: Grinding, Spicing & Curing

Step 1

Ice down all grinder parts, meat tubs, and water vessels in a large tub for 30 minutes before cutting.

Step 2

Blend recipe weights of lean beef, pork, and hard fat cuts uniformly.

Step 3

Run a rapid coarse grind using a 3/8-inch plate directly into the iced bus tub.

Step 4

Evenly distribute Bulk Kosher Salt, Curing Salt, and dry spices across grind.

Step 5

Dry mix with hands for 2 minutes to completely integrate the cure into the meat.

Step 6

Cover tightly and store in walk-in for mandatory overnight cure window.

Day 2: Mixing, Stuffing & Linking

Step 7

Submerge stuffer parts in ice bath for 30 minutes.

Step 8

Prep custom add-in ingredients per selected recipe.

Step 9

Whisk ice water binder mixture with powdered milk and pepper.

Step 10

Pour binder over cured meat and execute heavy manual mixing for 3 mins.

Step 11

Incorporate remaining ice water, cheese cubes, and add-ins.

Step 12

Knead aggressively for 3-5 minutes until meat paste turns sticky.

Step 13

Flush natural hog casings, slide onto horn, and pack cylinder tightly.

Step 14

Extrude onto wet table, pinch at 6-inch intervals, and twist links.

Step 15

Hang links on racks in walk-in for 2+ hours to form pellicle before smoking.

  • 10 Cups Extra Water (~5.0 lbs liquid binder)
  • 5 Cups Powdered Milk (~1.25 lbs dry binder)

✅ Batch Yield Verification: 50 lbs base meat + liquids & binders = ~62.5 lbs Total Stuffed Sausage Batch Weight. At ~0.38 lbs (6 oz) per stuffed link, 1 batch yields ~150 to 160 links. Therefore, a 148 link target = Exactly 1 Full 50-lb Base Meat Batch!
