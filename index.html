  • The Baseline Trend: By analyzing historical sales data through our Standard Forecasting Model, our pit operations maintain a daily average baseline of ~$2,330/day (with normal weekdays averaging ~$1,620/day and peak weekend surges hitting ~$4,250/day).
  • Actionable Insight - Meat Procurement: The forecast dictates prepping ~165 lbs raw brisket (~66 lbs cooked yield) and ~85 lbs raw pork shoulder (~34 lbs cooked yield) [~250 lbs total raw / ~100 lbs total cooked meat] for peak Saturday. Because brisket and pork lose ~60% of their weight during the 14-hour smoke, purchasing exactly the projected raw amounts mathematically ensures we hit our target sell-out time right at 9:00 PM closing without premature sellouts or expensive food waste.

🌧️ Weather Impact on Sales

Step-by-Step Reasoning: How Weather Affects Sales

  • Precipitation Impact (+$97.37/mm): Blending Open-Meteo Jacksonville rainfall data with daily POS transactions reveals that moderate rain shifts ordering behavior to higher-ticket family bundles for at-home dining.
  • Extreme Heat (>90°F): Outdoor patio dining drops significantly during Florida heat waves. Dine-in volume concentrates heavily at the indoor air-conditioned tables, and overflow customers shift toward quick takeout or curbside pickup rather than sitting outside.
  • Operational Choice - Weather Adjustments: Since we avoid margin-killing third-party delivery, leverage heavy rain days by promoting "Rainy Day Vacuum-Sealed Freezer Packs." On days when rainfall exceeds 10mm, reduce walk-in hot brisket prep by 15%, but immediately vacuum-seal the surplus and promote it on social media for customers to stock their freezers. During extreme heat, shift front-of-house staff to expedite curbside pickup to keep customers in their air-conditioned cars.

  • Jaguars Game Day (3.5x Multiplier): EverBank Stadium home games trigger a massive 3.5x revenue multiplier ($17.4k vs $4.9k).   • Florida-Georgia Game (5.0x Multiplier): The "World's Largest Outdoor Cocktail Party" brings an unprecedented weekend surge. Actionable Insight: Because RV City opens days in advance, demand spikes on Thursday and Friday, not just Saturday. Actionable Insight: Shift Cook #3 (Evening Pit Lead) to purely front-of-house expediting during these two hours to clear the line quickly, holding brisket hot in the warmer instead of cutting to order.
